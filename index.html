HOW TO QUALIFY

Evidence beats opinion.

A strong challenge is specific, sourced, and addresses the strongest counterargument. Follow these steps.

  1. Identify one specific GEI statement or interpretation you are challenging.
  2. Reference the relevant Genesis passage, Hebrew language element, or engineering claim.
  3. Provide reliable, checkable evidence that contradicts the claim.
  4. Explain why the contradiction materially weakens or disproves that part of GEI.
  5. Address the strongest reasonable response to your argument.

EVIDENCE BEATS OPINION.

Disagreement, ridicule, unsupported assertions, and arguments that merely offer another interpretation do not qualify by themselves.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Who decides whether GEI has been disproved?

A verified challenge must be assessed by an independent reviewer — not Wilbert Bouie Jr. and not the person submitting the challenge.

  • Independence: The reviewer must disclose relevant financial, professional, personal, or research conflicts and recuse themselves where impartiality could reasonably be questioned.
  • Evidence-first decision: The reviewer evaluates the exact GEI claim, the submitter's sources, the reasoning, and any written response from Wilbert.
  • Public explanation: Every verified or rejected determination receives a short written rationale with sources, the specific claim examined, and the review date.
  • Meaningful scope: A finding applies to the specific claim actually refuted. It does not automatically disprove every part of GEI.
  • Scoreboard integrity: Only after the written determination is published is the issue titled [GEI CHALLENGE] [VERIFIED] and counted as a verified challenge.
Reviewer appointment: No reviewer is being represented as appointed yet. The reviewer's name, relevant qualifications, independence statement, and any disclosed conflicts must be published on this page before the prize contest officially opens.

HOW THE SCOREBOARD CHANGES

Submission is not the same as proof.

The scoreboard tracks two separate things: how many challenges have been submitted, and how many have survived independent review. Here is how the process works.

1
Submission
An entry is received and listed publicly. This does not affect the streak.
2
Under Review
The argument is examined against the published criteria.
3
Verified Challenge
The independent reviewer publishes a reasoned finding that the evidence materially refutes a defined GEI claim.
4
Streak Status
The streak remains active until a verified challenge is recorded. A verified refutation changes the status to STREAK STOPPED.

The scoreboard counts only public GitHub issues with titles beginning [GEI CHALLENGE]. A verified result must be explicitly marked [GEI CHALLENGE] [VERIFIED] after the independent decision is published.
